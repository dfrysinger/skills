# Environment-fit ledger

- Candidate-observable: exact outcome, VM boundary, proposed service/store,
  claimed failure, simplest local alternative, and absence of an external
  proof obligation.
- Judge-observable: completed skill invocation receipts and whether the gate
  result was applied.
- Live or post-environment facts: none.
- Gate: the case tests the reviewed shared-gate contract and does not itself
  authorize the proposed expansion.
